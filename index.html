About Me

My Journey into Web Development

I'm Joseph Emmanuel N. Calvo, an aspiring front-end developer with a passion for creating interactive and functional websites. This portfolio serves as both a showcase of my work and a record of my continuous growth in web development.

I relearned everything from scratch through self-directed learning on YouTube. I discovered a comprehensive 48-hour course titled "How to Become a Full-Stack Developer from Scratch" on freeCodeCamp.org, which reignited my passion for web development.

I've also been learning extensively from SuperSimpleDev on YouTube, whose clear and practical tutorials have helped me understand complex concepts in a simple, hands-on way. His teaching style has been instrumental in building my confidence as a developer.

I'm deeply grateful to Scrimba, freeCodeCamp.org, and SuperSimpleDev for providing these incredible learning opportunities.
