Our Rich History

From humble beginnings to educational excellence - the story of SNHS

1948 - The Beginning

The dream of having a secondary school in Sagay City after World War II was realized during the term of the late Mayor Jose B. Puey, Sr. and Vice Mayor Amalio R. Cueva, Sr. The first secondary school was formally opened in school year 1948-1949.

Growth & Development

From its humble beginning with 218 enrollees, SNHS has grown tremendously. Today, our 800,000 square-meter campus houses 144 dedicated teachers and 5,022 students, making us one of the largest schools in the region.

Leadership Legacy

SNHS pays gratitude to the ten principals who have served with dedication. Under the current leadership of Mrs. Vilia G. Tupas, the school continues to thrive and maintain its commitment to educational excellence.

Academic Programs

Comprehensive education programs designed to prepare students for higher education and future careers.

🎓 Junior High School Programs (Grades 7-10)

Regular Class

Comprehensive curriculum covering core subjects with various programs to develop students' interests and abilities.

  • Core subjects: Math, Science, English, Filipino
  • Social Studies and Values Education
  • Technology and Livelihood Education
  • Physical Education and Health
  • Arts and Music programs

SPED (Special Education)

Specialized program for gifted, talented, and differently-abled students with modified curricula and facilities.

  • Modified school curricula and programs
  • Special services and physical facilities
  • Individualized learning approaches
  • Inclusive education environment

STEM Program

Unified academic program focusing on Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics with ICT and Research.

  • Interdisciplinary STEM approach
  • ICT and Research subjects
  • Real-world applications
  • Cohesive learning paradigm

SPA (Special Program in the Arts)

Explores students' artistic inclinations in media arts, visual arts, theatre, dance, and music.

  • Media and Visual Arts
  • Theatre Arts and Dance
  • Music and Performance
  • Heritage appreciation and artistic excellence

STVEP (Technical-Vocational)

Strengthened Technical-Vocational Education Program preparing graduates for work and entrepreneurship.

  • Work-ready graduates
  • Entrepreneurship development
  • Sustainable development focus
  • Globally recognized program

OHSP (Open High School)

Modular program for working students, supported by the Local School Board with Saturday classes.

  • Flexible Saturday schedule
  • Modular learning approach
  • For working students
  • Local School Board support

🎯 Senior High School Programs (Grades 11-12)

STEM Track

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics track for students pursuing careers in technical fields.

  • Advanced Mathematics and Statistics
  • Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
  • Research and Inquiry
  • Pre-Calculus and Basic Calculus
  • Computer Programming basics

ABM Track

Accountancy, Business, and Management track preparing students for business and entrepreneurship.

  • Fundamentals of Accountancy
  • Business Mathematics and Ethics
  • Organization and Management
  • Business Finance and Marketing
  • Entrepreneurship development

HUMSS Track

Humanities and Social Sciences track for students interested in social sciences and liberal arts.

  • Creative Writing and Reading
  • Philippine Politics and Governance
  • World Religions and Belief Systems
  • Trends, Networks and Critical Thinking
  • Community Engagement and Research

TVL Track

Technical-Vocational-Livelihood track focusing on practical skills and career preparation.

  • Technical and vocational skills
  • Livelihood and entrepreneurship
  • Industry-relevant training
  • Work immersion programs
